Yes, Tone.js is very good for recording and creating audio on the web, offering a high-level abstraction over the native Web Audio API, making complex tasks like instrument creation, synchronization (DAW-like transport), and effects easier and more musical, though achieving sample-accurate punch-in/out recording requires deeper integration with the underlying Web Audio API's MediaStream Recording. It provides synths, samplers, effects, and transport controls, simplifying browser-based audio development for developers and musicians alike, with excellent documentation and demos available.
